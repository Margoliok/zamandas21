15 years — starting from one yard

It began with one basement club in 2011. Today: 7 clubs in Astana and almost 5,000 children.

Leadership and teachers

Lyazzat Alshinova

Director of the foundation and author of the family yard club idea.

Our teachers are coaches, musicians, educators and psychologists. Many started as volunteers: our English teacher first came as a volunteer, and a native-speaker volunteer works with the children twice a week.

History

From 2011 to 2026

  1. 2011
    First club in a basement on Abylai Khan St, opened with the Bota Foundation. 5 activities, about 100 children.
  2. 2018
    Samruk-Kazyna Trust becomes a partner; together we hold a city Family Day festival.
  3. 2021
    5 clubs, about 2,500 children, 30+ activities. Classes moved online during lockdown.
  4. 2024
    6 clubs, 3,000+ children. In September a youth coworking space for ages 14–35 opened.
  5. 2025
    7 clubs, 3,500+ children. The main sponsor ended funding; for months the clubs were kept going by teachers and volunteers.
  6. 2026
    15 years, almost 5,000 children, 22 activities.

Completed projects

More than activities

  • Youth coworking — since 2024, a free space for ages 14–35: training, speaking clubs, coaching, a psychologist.
  • Anti-bullying in schools — with the Czech Embassy: 90 psychologists from across Kazakhstan trained online, work in 4 schools.
  • Yard hockey league — 9 years; 100 children equipped through a grant, matches at the rink of School No. 32.
  • Skills for women — training in 9 professions with the Civic Initiatives Support Centre; some graduates started businesses.
