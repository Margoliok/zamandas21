Key facts

A yard club next door

Zamandas 21 is a network of family yard clubs in Astana. The first club opened in 2011 in a basement on Abylai Khan Street, with 5 activities and about 100 children.

Today there are 7 clubs, 22 activities and almost 5,000 children. Classes run all year round, including summer. The founder of the family yard club idea and director of the foundation is Lyazzat Alshinova.

Goals and objectives

A chance for every child

  • Accessible development. Quality activities free of charge for children from vulnerable families.
  • Strong families. Psychological support, courses and training for parents.
  • A safe place. After school, children are with caring adults rather than on the street.
  • Youth support. Coworking, training and a psychologist for ages 14–35.

How we work

For children and adults

  • Activities and sections — arts, mind games, languages, sport.
  • Psychological service — for children, teens and parents, including anonymously.
  • “Happy Family” club — assessment, seminars, parenting courses.
  • Skills for mothers — sewing, cooking and 7 more professions.
  • Projects — yard hockey league, anti-bullying, youth coworking.

What makes us unique

We work with the whole family, not just the child

While a child is at an activity, their parent can talk to a psychologist, join a training session or learn a new profession.
